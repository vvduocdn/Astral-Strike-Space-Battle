## Description (English)

**Blast through the cosmos in the ultimate space shooter experience!**

Astral Strike: Space Battle is an action-packed arcade shooter that puts you in command of a powerful starship. Defend the galaxy against waves of alien invaders, upgrade your weapons, and become the ultimate space warrior!

**INTENSE GAMEPLAY**
• Fast-paced arcade action with smooth touch controls
• 20 challenging levels with increasing difficulty
• Epic boss battles every 5 levels
• Auto-fire system lets you focus on dodging and strategy

**POWERFUL WEAPONS**
• 5 unique weapon types: Laser, Spread, Missile, Plasma, Thunder
• Upgrade your weapons to unleash devastating firepower
• Collect power-ups to boost your combat abilities

**DEADLY ENEMIES**
• Face 5 different enemy types with unique behaviors
• Basic, Fast, Tank, Shooter, and Zigzag enemies
• Massive boss encounters that will test your skills

**POWER-UPS & ABILITIES**
• Health restore to keep you in the fight
• Shield for temporary invincibility
• Weapon upgrades for extra damage
• Speed boost for quick maneuvers
• Score multiplier for high score hunters
• Bombs to clear the screen in emergencies

**PROGRESSION SYSTEM**
• Unlock and upgrade 6 unique ship designs
• Permanent upgrades: Health, Speed, Shield
• Achievement system with rewards
• Daily login rewards with streak bonuses

**FEATURES**
• Stunning space visuals with parallax backgrounds
• Immersive sound effects and background music
• Works offline - play anywhere, anytime
• No forced ads during gameplay
• Suitable for all ages

Download Astral Strike: Space Battle now and begin your journey to become the galaxy's greatest defender!
